Reviewed November 2, 2026 (Asia/Dubai). Target November 8. Architecture only: **no legal policy text is supplied or approved**. Do not copy the inactive Safaa clinic's policies. This checklist is not a legal determination or compliance certification.

## Observed implementation and limits

- Phone/WhatsApp/email are links (`lib/clinic.ts`, contact actions and footer). There is no site-side contact submission, booking form, patient database, newsletter signup backend or account/login system in this Next application. The appointment modal offers outbound contact actions, not a data-entry form. Existing static testimonials are displayed from translations, not submitted through this website.
- The active email destination is `mail@baghdadmedicalcenter.ae`; Google Workspace and recovery of historical email are owner-confirmed. `mailto:` opens the visitor's configured mail client; this site does not itself send messages. No mailbox, recovered email or patient information was accessed. Handling of subsequent email/phone/WhatsApp communications needs clinic confirmation.
- Home and Contact include a lazy Google Maps iframe (`components/sections/contact.tsx`, `lib/clinic.ts`), localized by `hl`. Its source is `https://www.google.com/maps?q=24.9146055,55.7757416&hl=…&z=17&output=embed`; referrer policy is `no-referrer-when-downgrade`. Loading the iframe makes an external request without a consent gate. Google may redirect/load additional resources inside its frame; this audit does not establish all third-party cookie/storage behavior across browsers, sign-in states or regions. Outbound business-listing, directions and reviews links also lead to Google.
- WhatsApp (`wa.me`), Instagram and Facebook are outbound links, not embedded feeds or tracking pixels. Calls use `tel:`. Visitors may leave this site/use another application; the clinic must confirm its processes and supplier relationships rather than claim no data is processed.
- Public unauthenticated HTTP observations on November 2: EN/AR Home and Contact return `NEXT_LOCALE` with `Path=/; SameSite=lax` and no observed Expires/Max-Age (session cookie). Values were not retained. Locale routing uses next-intl's default language preference behavior (`i18n/routing.ts`, `proxy.ts`). Cookie duration/purpose must be reviewed against the actual launch setup, not an invented retention period.
- Source search of app/components/hooks/i18n/lib found no application `localStorage`/`sessionStorage` calls, analytics SDK, Google Tag Manager/gtag, advertising pixel or remote executable script tag. Theme state is React memory only, not persisted (`components/providers/theme-provider.tsx`). JSON-LD script tags contain data, not analytics. `next/font` and optimized images are served as local Next assets in the inspected HTML.
- Public HTML on the four inspected demo URLs referenced same-origin `/_next/` executable scripts and the Maps iframe. Prior read-only Vercel deployment views showed Web Analytics and Speed Insights not enabled; this is a dated observation, not proof of every account-level integration or future production setting. The Vercel toolbar may run on authenticated/protected previews; distinguish it from ordinary public production browsing.
- Demo hosting is Vercel. Public requests necessarily reach hosting/CDN systems, which can receive request/network information. Exact logs, access, retention, processing locations, agreements and optional integrations were **not** inspected in account settings and must not be invented. The separately hosted official site's observed Cloudflare DNS/proxy setup is documented in `DOMAIN_MIGRATION_AUDIT.md`; it does not establish the future deployment's data practices.

These are source/limited HTTP observations, not an exhaustive browser-cookie, vendor-contract or legal audit. No cookie values, credentials, private configuration, messages or personal submissions were collected. Demo noindex is search protection, not access control or a privacy guarantee.

## Prepared code and publication behavior

`lib/content/legal-documents.ts` has empty typed Privacy, Terms and Cookie slots; all are draft and applicability `needs-review`. One server template renders plain text, semantic headings, section links, breadcrumbs and shared contact/footer information. No raw HTML, policy generator, new script, cookie banner, form or dependency is introduced.

Footer props contain only approved public titles/paths.

The same gate controls page rendering, future footer discovery and `getEligiblePages()` sitemap/metadata eligibility. Header routes support page-preserving locale switching without adding legal navigation items. Future approved official pages use existing self-canonical/reciprocal hreflang logic; demo/preview noindex and exact-host protections are unchanged. No last-modified/effective dates are invented. No separate legal schema or compliance claims are added.
